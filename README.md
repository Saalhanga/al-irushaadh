# Al Irushaadh

💡 Lovable App Prompt: United Islamic Society Fundraising Platform

Mission Statement

I want to build a multilingual fundraising website that helps the Muslim community contribute to the construction of the United Islamic Society office project. It should feel official, trustworthy, calm, and community-driven, while expressing dignity, transparency, and unity.

Project Name

UIS Office 1320

Target Audience

 Members of the local Muslim community who want to support the office project

 Families and individuals who want to sponsor a small part (1 square foot concept)

 Donors who prefer official, transparent Islamic organizations before contributing

Core Features and Pages

✅ Homepage

The homepage should feel official and respectful — similar to an Islamic organization website rather than a commercial fundraising page.

Layout flow:

 Hero section with the building image and a calm headline like
“Build the United Islamic Society Office Together”

 Main call-to-action: Sponsor 1 Square Foot – 1320 MVR

 Donation progress bar showing percentage completed

 Quick language switcher (Dhivehi / English / Arabic)

 Short section explaining the project and its importance for the community

Emotionally, the homepage should feel trustworthy and dignified rather than emotional or aggressive.

✅ Sponsor a Square Foot Page

This is the core feature of the website.

Function:

 Users can sponsor 1 square foot for 1320 MVR

 Show progress visually (for example: 320 / 1320 square feet completed)

 Clean donation card layout with clear price and purpose

 Bank account section displayed clearly (with percentage progress next to it)

Emotion:
It should feel simple and respectful — no pressure tactics, just a clear opportunity to contribute.

✅ Project Details Page

This page explains:

 What the building will be used for

 Why the office is important for the community

 Photos or architectural images (like the one you shared)

 A clear explanation of the square-foot sponsorship idea

Emotion:
This page should build trust and confidence in the project.

✅ CMS Admin Panel (Backend)

A simple content management system where the organization can:

 Edit text in Dhivehi, English, and Arabic

 Update donation progress manually

 Change bank account details

 Upload new photos of the building progress

 Edit homepage sections without coding

Tech Stack

 Frontend: Vite + TypeScript + React + shadcn/ui + Tailwind CSS

 Backend & Storage: Lovable Cloud (use this for CMS + donation progress storage)

 Auth: Admin login using email/password

Design Guidelines (based on design-tips.md )

Emotional Thesis

Feels like an official Islamic organization that is calm, trustworthy, and community-driven — respectful, minimal, and dignified rather than modern-startup style.

Typography

Following the “start with feeling, not features” principle

 Headings: Elegant and calm Islamic-friendly sans-serif style

 Body text: Very clean and readable with generous spacing

 Arabic text should feel respectful and properly spaced

 Use larger heading spacing to make the site feel official and calm

Typography hierarchy:

 H1: Strong but calm (not bold startup style)

 H2: Structured and formal

 Body: Clean and readable with relaxed spacing

Color System

The colors should reflect trust, Islam, and official organization tone.

Suggested palette:

 Primary: Deep green (#1F5E4A) → represents trust and Islamic identity

 Background: Soft white (#F7F8F7) → calm and respectful

 Accent: Gold-soft beige (#C9A96A) → adds dignity without looking luxury-commercial

 Text: Dark charcoal (#1A1A1A)

Color logic:
Soft, calm, and respectful — not bright or modern-startup colors.

Layout & Spacing

Based on the “design for emotional moments” principle

 Clean vertical layout

 Lots of breathing space between sections

 Center-aligned hero text to feel official

 Donation card clearly separated from content

 Progress bar large and easy to understand

Spacing should make the website feel calm and respectful, not crowded.

Motion & Interactions

Use motion gently — kindness in design.

Examples:

 Progress bar fills slowly when the page loads

 Buttons fade softly instead of jumping

 Language switch transitions smoothly

 No aggressive animations

Emotion:
The site should feel peaceful and respectful.

Voice & Microcopy

Tone should sound official but warm.

Examples:

 “Support the United Islamic Society Office Project”

 “Sponsor 1 Square Foot and be part of the community effort”

 “May Allah reward your contribution”

 “Your support helps build a space for the community”

Accessibility

 Strong color contrast

 Clear Arabic / Dhivehi readability

 Large buttons for older donors

 Very simple navigation

Optional AI Feature

A small assistant that helps users understand:

 How the square-foot sponsorship works

 How to donate using the bank account

 What the project will be used for

Personality:
A calm and respectful guide, not a chatbot that feels commercial.

Design Integrity Review

 Does the design feel official and trustworthy? ✔️

 Does it feel community-driven rather than commercial? ✔️

 Do typography, colors, and layout reflect calm Islamic dignity? ✔️

 Would donors feel comfortable and confident contributing? ✔️
